About Hemant Singh (हेमंत कुमार सिंह) — Motilal Nehru College, DU

Hemant Singh (also known as Hemant Kumar Singh / हेमंत कुमार सिंह) serves as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Hindi at Motilal Nehru College, University of Delhi (DU). He was permanently appointed following official university selection committee notifications (Ref. No. MNC/BJM/2023/814, dated 28 October 2023).

His academic scholarship focuses on 21st-century Hindi literary discourses, Hindi cinema and visual culture, environmental ecology in modern Hindi poetry (notably Baba Nagarjun), and research methodology in Hindi studies.

Verified Research & Peer-Reviewed Publications
